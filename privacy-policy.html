Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Pack Break Queue application ("the App") handles data.

1. Who this covers

The App is a private tool operated by a single individual seller for their own TikTok Shop. It is not distributed to or used by other sellers or the general public.

2. What data is accessed

The App connects to the operator's own TikTok Shop account using TikTok's official API and OAuth authorization. It reads order data belonging to that shop, including order line items (product name, quantity) and basic buyer identifiers needed to display an order in a queue (such as a name or order reference).

3. What data is stored, and where

Order data read from TikTok Shop is stored locally, on the operator's own computer and/or browser, for the purpose of displaying and managing the live queue. It is not uploaded to any third-party server, sold, shared, or used for advertising.

4. What data is not collected

The App does not collect payment information, passwords, or any TikTok account data beyond what is needed to read order line items from the operator's own shop.

5. Data retention

Order data is retained locally only as long as needed to manage the current or recent queue, and can be cleared at any time by the operator.

6. Third parties

The App does not share data with any third party. It communicates only with TikTok's official API.

7. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated at any time.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the operator via TikTok Shop.
